What it is

A tool for the parts of the job that repeat.

Independent contractors lose hours every week to the same mechanical steps — reformatting, staging, checking, and submitting work in the same shape over and over. Cohandy takes those defined, repetitive steps off their plate and runs them reliably, leaving the judgment and the substance to the person. It's billed as a simple weekly subscription per user.

How it works

Three steps, then it runs in the background.

STEP 01

Connect

A contractor sets up Cohandy once against the routine workflow they want handled.

STEP 02

Automate

Cohandy runs the repetitive, rule-based steps of that workflow on their behalf.

STEP 03

Review

The person stays in control — reviewing output and keeping the work that needs them.
